# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to be respectful, constructive, and
welcoming. Participate in good faith, assume technical disagreements can be
resolved, and focus criticism on code and decisions rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, and publication of private information without consent
are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or edit contributions, limit participation, or
take other action needed to keep project spaces safe. Report conduct concerns
privately to the project maintainers or through the repository's private
security/contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
